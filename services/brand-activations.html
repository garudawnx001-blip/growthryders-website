Questions people ask

What is a brand activation?

A brand activation is an on-ground campaign where people meet a brand directly: they see the product, try it, take part in an activity or talk to the team, instead of only seeing an advertisement.

Where does Growthryders run brand activations in Bengaluru?

In corporate and tech parks such as the Bagmane business parks, in malls and public spaces, on campuses, in residential communities and at dealer and retail locations.

Which brands has Growthryders run activations for?

TVS Apache across five Bagmane business parks and at the Tu Race Laga corporate run, and Royal Enfield at Bagmane Constellation Business Park through the Bikers Bay community.
