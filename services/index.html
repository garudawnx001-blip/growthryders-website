Sponsorship Sales & Revenue Management

More on this service

Turning IPs, Events & B2B Platforms into Revenue Opportunities.

We help Intellectual Property (IP) owners, event organisers and B2B conference platforms unlock sponsorship opportunities through a structured, technology-enabled sales ecosystem.

Combining AI-powered research and prospecting with human-led engagement, relationship management and commercial negotiations, we manage the complete sponsorship sales lifecycle.

Our approach builds a continuous sales pipeline, from identifying potential sponsors and mapping decision-makers to personalised outreach, proposal development, negotiations and closure.

Our capabilities include:

  • Sponsorship Strategy & Revenue Planning
  • IP and Event Monetisation
  • AI-enabled Brand Identification & Prospecting
  • Industry-wise Sponsor Database Development
  • Decision-maker Mapping & Lead Qualification
  • Personalised Outreach & Sales Engagement
  • Sponsorship Proposal Development
  • Negotiations & Commercial Closure
  • CRM-based Pipeline & Follow-up Management
  • Sponsorship Revenue Tracking & Reporting

Our Operating Model: AI Intelligence + Human Expertise

We combine the speed and intelligence of AI with the relationship-building and negotiation capabilities of experienced sales professionals to create a continuous, opportunity-driven sponsorship pipeline.

From individual events and proprietary IPs to large-scale B2B conclaves, conferences and industry platforms, we operate as an extended sponsorship sales and revenue management partner.
