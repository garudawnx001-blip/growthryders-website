Sponsorship Development & Event Execution Case Study

TVS APACHE PRESENTS – TU RACE LAGA

A Bagmane Tech Parks Corporate Running Initiative

Conceptualisation, Sponsorship Development & Event Execution by Growthryders

1. Project Overview

The Tu Race Laga initiative was conceptualised as a corporate running event for employees and the wider community across Bagmane Tech Parks.

The objective was to create an engaging fitness-oriented event that encouraged participation, promoted a healthy lifestyle and provided brands with an opportunity to connect with a corporate audience.

The event featured three running categories:

  • 10K Run
  • 5K Run
  • 3K Run

The central theme of the event was “Challenging Yourself.”

What began as a corporate running event requirement evolved into a multi-brand sponsorship property through strategic brand integration and sponsorship development by Growthryders.

2. Project Initiation – Bagmane REIT

The initiative originated from Bagmane REIT’s requirement to organise a corporate running event across its technology parks.

The requirement was to create an event that would:

  • Encourage fitness and physical well-being among employees.
  • Bring together the corporate community through a common activity.
  • Create an engaging and professionally organised running experience.
  • Provide opportunities for brands to associate with the event.

Growthryders took forward the event conceptualisation, sponsorship development and organisational responsibilities.

3. Event Conceptualisation & Property Development

Growthryders developed the event around the concept of challenging oneself through running.

The event was structured into three categories to encourage participation from individuals with different fitness levels.

Event Categories

CategoryPurpose
10K RunFor experienced and competitive runners
5K RunFor regular fitness enthusiasts
3K RunFor beginners and recreational participants

The objective was to position the event as more than just a running activity. It was developed as a corporate community engagement property with potential for brand partnerships and sponsorship opportunities.

4. Sponsorship Strategy & Brand Outreach

Once the event concept and property were established, Growthryders initiated the sponsorship outreach process.

The strategy was to identify brands that aligned with the event’s fitness, lifestyle, performance and corporate audience.

Rather than approaching brands with generic sponsorship opportunities, the focus was on identifying relevant brand campaigns and integrating them into the event identity.

This approach created an opportunity to develop a sponsorship structure where different brands could participate through clearly defined partnership categories.

5. Strategic Brand Integration – TVS Apache

One of the key developments in the sponsorship journey was the integration of TVS Apache’s latest campaign, “TU RACE LAGA.”

Growthryders identified an opportunity to connect TVS Apache’s campaign positioning with the running event.

The campaign’s racing and performance-oriented identity aligned naturally with the event’s running concept and its central theme of challenging oneself.

The proposed integration was accepted by TVS Apache, enabling the event to be presented as:

TVS APACHE PRESENTS – TU RACE LAGA

This integration brought a strong brand identity to the event and created a distinctive association between the automotive brand’s campaign and the corporate running property.

The initiative demonstrated how an existing brand campaign could be strategically integrated into an independently developed event property.

6. Sponsorship Architecture & Brand Partnerships

Following the title integration, the event was developed into a multi-brand sponsorship ecosystem.

The participating brands were associated with different sponsorship categories based on their respective roles.

The sponsorship structure provided multiple brands with opportunities to associate with the event while maintaining a unified event identity.

Each partner contributed to a different aspect of the overall event ecosystem.

7. Sponsorship Flow – From Requirement to Execution

The overall sponsorship development process followed this sequence:

  1. Stage 1 – Client RequirementBagmane REIT identified the requirement for a corporate running event.
  2. Stage 2 – Event ConceptualisationGrowthryders developed the event concept around the theme of challenging yourself, with 10K, 5K and 3K running categories.
  3. Stage 3 – Property DevelopmentThe event was structured as a corporate running property with opportunities for brand association and sponsorship.
  4. Stage 4 – Brand OutreachGrowthryders approached potential sponsors and explored relevant brand partnerships.
  5. Stage 5 – Strategic Campaign IdentificationTVS Apache's Tu Race Laga campaign was identified as an appropriate fit for the running event.
  6. Stage 6 – Title Brand IntegrationThe proposed association was accepted, and the event was positioned as TVS Apache Presents – Tu Race Laga.
  7. Stage 7 – Multi-Brand Sponsorship DevelopmentAdditional partners were associated with the event across banking, ticketing, nutrition and protein categories.
  8. Stage 8 – Event Coordination & ExecutionGrowthryders coordinated the event ecosystem, working with Bagmane REIT and the participating sponsors.

9. Growthryders’ Strategic Contribution

The key contribution of Growthryders was the transformation of a corporate event requirement into a structured sponsorship-led event property.

The approach involved three important dimensions:

A. Event Property Development

Instead of treating the initiative as only an event-management requirement, Growthryders developed a property that could attract relevant brands and sponsorship opportunities.

B. Campaign-Led Brand Integration

The integration of TVS Apache’s Tu Race Laga campaign demonstrated the value of connecting an existing brand campaign with a relevant event concept.

C. Multi-Brand Sponsorship Ecosystem

The addition of partners across different categories helped establish a broader sponsorship structure around the event.

10. Key Takeaways

The Tu Race Laga initiative demonstrates the importance of strategic event conceptualisation and sponsorship development.

Key learnings from the initiative include:

  1. An event can be transformed into a sponsorship property through the right positioning and conceptualisation.
  2. Existing brand campaigns can create meaningful integration opportunities with relevant events.
  3. Sponsorship outreach becomes more effective when brands are approached with a clear association opportunity.
  4. Multiple brands can participate in one event through distinct sponsorship categories.
  5. Strong coordination between the event organiser, client and sponsors is essential for successful execution.

11. Conclusion

The TVS Apache Presents – Tu Race Laga initiative represents a strategic approach to developing a corporate running event into a multi-brand sponsorship property.

Beginning with Bagmane REIT’s requirement for a corporate running initiative, Growthryders developed the event concept, identified relevant sponsorship opportunities and proposed the integration of TVS Apache’s Tu Race Laga campaign.

The successful brand association, along with the participation of Axis Bank, iRaces, Milky Mist and Protein Chef, helped establish a structured sponsorship ecosystem.

The initiative reflects Growthryders’ approach of combining event conceptualisation, brand alignment, sponsorship development and event execution to create meaningful brand and community engagement opportunities.
