Questions people ask us

What does Growthryders do?

Growthryders is a brand activation agency and through-the-line marketing company based in Bengaluru (Bangalore). One team plans and runs brand activations, experiential marketing, media, corporate events, digital marketing and technology-led experiences.

Where is Growthryders based?

Growthryders is based in Bengaluru (Bangalore), Karnataka, India.

What kinds of brand activations does Growthryders run?

Corporate park activations, mall and public space promotions, product sampling, roadshows, campus and residential activations, and brand launches.

Which services does Growthryders offer?

Brand activations and TTL marketing; Digital marketing; Experiential marketing; Media solutions; Corporate events and engagement; Sponsorship Sales & Revenue Management; Technology-led experiences; Film and content production.

Which brands has Growthryders been on the ground with?

TVS Apache, Royal Enfield, Bagmane REIT, Axis Bank, Milky Mist, Kauvery Hospital, Protein Chef and iRaces.

What are Runcle and Bikers Bay?

They are platforms Growthryders owns. Runcle is a fitness and wellness platform where running meets cycling, for runners, cyclists and corporate teams. Bikers Bay is a motorcycle community with rides, meets and brand collaborations.
